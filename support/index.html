Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your Mac. Booster Books has no accounts and doesn't send your records anywhere. Nobody else, including the developer, can see them.

How do I back up my records or hand off to the next treasurer?

Open Setup and choose Export History. It saves a single file with every year, bank upload, receipt and correction, plus a spreadsheet of all transactions. The next treasurer uses Import History on their Mac. The app also keeps automatic local backups, listed under Setup.

Which bank files can I import?

Booster Books reads the CSV transaction download from most banks. It shows a preview and checks the running balance before anything is imported. If your bank's file doesn't read correctly, email me and, if you can, attach a copy with the account details removed.

How do I clear the sample data?

Open Setup and choose Clear Sample Data. Your own records are never touched.

Is Booster Books tax or accounting advice?

No. It's a bookkeeping aid. For tax or legal questions, please consult a qualified professional.
